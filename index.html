About Us

Welcome to ARP Knots!

We're a trio of college friends who realized our crochet addiction was getting out of hand and decided to turn it into tiny, squishable desk buddies for you.

When we aren't studying for finals or cramming between classes, you can find us tangled up in pastel yarn, crocheting creatures like Stingy, Whale Puffs, and we even accept commissions of your choosing. Everything you see here is stitched, stuffed, and packaged by our six hands.

No mass production, no factories—just three students making cute, budget-friendly companions from scratch. Thanks for supporting our small business and keeping our yarn stash alive!
